# Privacy policy: Caption Scribe

Last updated 2026-08-13.

This add-on collects nothing, transmits nothing, and contacts no server of its
own. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no remote
configuration.

## What it accesses

While you are on a youtube.com page, the add-on reads:

- The video's public metadata: title, channel, duration, description, and
  chapter markers.
- The caption data the page itself fetches from YouTube.

## Where that data goes

Into the document it produces, and nowhere else. Processing happens entirely
inside your browser. The result reaches your clipboard or your downloads folder
only when you click a button that asks for it.

The add-on makes no network requests to any host other than youtube.com, and the
only requests it makes there are for caption data on the video you are viewing.

## What is stored

Your formatting preferences, held in Firefox's extension storage. These sync
across your Firefox profiles if you have Firefox Sync enabled, because that is
how Firefox's own storage works. They contain settings only, never transcript
content, video history, or anything identifying.

Uninstalling the add-on removes them.

## Permissions and why they exist

- **Access to youtube.com** is required to read the page and its captions. The
  add-on runs on no other site.
- **Downloads** is required to save a transcript file when you choose a download
  rather than a copy.
- **Storage** is required to remember your formatting preferences.
